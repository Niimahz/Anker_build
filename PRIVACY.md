# Anker — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 14 November 2026  
**Project owner:** Niimahz

## What Anker does

Anker is an iOS browser and local download/archive manager. Its browser uses Apple's WebKit, and downloaded or imported files are managed in the application's local storage.

## Information handled by the app

- **Website activity:** WebKit necessarily communicates with the sites you visit. Those sites may receive your IP address, browser information, and other data and may set cookies or use tracking technologies under their own policies.
- **Downloads and files:** URLs, downloaded files, archive contents, and extracted folders are handled to provide the features you request. The app uses local storage for these operations.
- **Technical diagnostics:** Local error and diagnostic information may be displayed in the app for troubleshooting.

## Data sharing and collection

The inspected application source does not establish a publisher-operated analytics account, advertising account, or backend for collecting personal browsing history. **This is not a verified guarantee about all dependencies, operating-system services, third-party websites, or future releases.** The publisher must validate actual network traffic and App Store privacy disclosures before distribution.

Anker does not itself provide or control the privacy policies of websites you visit.

## Storage and deletion

Files are stored in the app's local container or in locations you select through iOS sharing/import features. You can delete downloaded archives and extracted folders using the app's controls. iOS backups, exported copies, and website-held data may persist separately.

## Children

Anker is not specifically designed for children. The publisher must establish age-rating and any legally required child-privacy practices before distribution.

## Security

The app uses platform-provided browser and file APIs, but no application can guarantee absolute security. Only open files and websites you trust.

Updates will be reflected by a revised effective date. For project inquiries, use https://github.com/Niimahz/Anker_build. A dedicated privacy contact address must be provided before commercial release.
